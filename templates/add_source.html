What happens

  1. Identify the document; detect type, date, draft/proposed status, authority.
  2. Extract every passage with page and section.
  3. Score passages against every indicator's concepts.
  4. Classify the level each passage can prove (intent → improvement), capped by document type.
  5. Create evidence records (status, strength, missing evidence, next artifact, owner).
  6. Skip duplicates; leave human-reviewed records untouched.
  7. Recalculate gaps and preliminary assessments.
Document type matters: reports, data, minutes and completion records can prove implementation and measurement. Plans prove intent. Policies, standards and webpages prove a stated process.
